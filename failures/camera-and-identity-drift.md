# Failure: Camera or identity drift

## Signature

The output has the right theme but a different camera angle, perspective, facial structure, skin tone, expression, or body geometry.

## Why it happens

Words such as “cinematic,” “beautiful,” or “editorial” can compete with preservation. The model invents a more stereotypical shot.

## Minimal repair

```text
Preserve the original camera height, angle, horizon, focal-length feel,
subject scale, head turn, expression, facial structure, and skin tone.
Apply the requested style only to [allowed materials, light, or background].
Do not beautify, reshape, recast, or invent a new shot.
```

## Verification

Compare identity and geometry before judging style. If the source is personal, keep the comparison private.

## Reusable rule

Identity and camera locks outrank aesthetic improvement.
