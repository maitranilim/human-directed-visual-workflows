# Failure: Artificial naturalism

## Signature

The output is technically polished but feels fake because of excessive saturation, clipped highlights, plastic surfaces, uniform smoothing, inconsistent noise, or dramatic relighting.

## Why it happens

“Enhance” is interpreted as maximum visible polish. The model removes the imperfections that made the source believable.

## Minimal repair

```text
Match the source lighting direction, contrast range, texture, sharpness, and
noise. Lower clipped highlights, use restrained saturation, preserve minor
asymmetry, and avoid beauty-filter smoothing or studio-perfect light.
```

## Verification

- materials retain local texture;
- bright regions keep detail;
- warm and cool areas remain plausible;
- subject edges do not look cut out;
- the output still resembles the source device or medium.

## Reusable rule

Believability often comes from preserved imperfections, not added polish.
