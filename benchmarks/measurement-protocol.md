# Measurement protocol

## Question

Do preservation locks, reusable fragments, and delta critiques reduce time and generations while holding the acceptance bar constant?

## Unit of analysis

One visual job with:

- one declared category;
- one brief;
- one quality threshold;
- one accepted output or explicit failure;
- a complete iteration record.

## Record

- model and tool;
- date;
- source class;
- prompt or fragment version;
- generations to acceptance;
- elapsed minutes;
- manual finishing minutes;
- primary failure per rejected iteration;
- quality-rubric score;
- whether a reusable fragment was used;
- whether rights and privacy passed.

## Comparison

For each task category, compare:

- baseline: ad hoc prompting;
- workflow: brief plus locks plus modular fragments plus delta critique.

Use the same deliverable type and acceptance threshold. Report medians, sample size, range, and any quality tradeoff.

## Claim threshold

- Fewer than 10 comparable jobs: case evidence only.
- 10 to 29 comparable jobs: preliminary internal finding.
- 30 or more comparable jobs: stronger descriptive evidence, still not a universal causal claim.

## Integrity rule

Leave unknown fields blank. Never reconstruct a number from memory merely to complete the table.
