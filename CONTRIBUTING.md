# Contributing

Contributions should make AI-assisted visual work more controllable, reproducible, or honestly evaluated.

## Include

- the visual goal and audience;
- preservation locks;
- the prompt fragments used;
- the observed failure;
- the smallest repair;
- output evidence that can legally and safely be shared;
- measured or explicitly unmeasured efficiency data.

## Exclude

- personal photographs without explicit publication intent;
- private or confidential source material;
- third-party meme templates or copyrighted reference images without rights;
- misleading claims of manual illustration;
- invented regeneration counts, time savings, or conversion results.

## New findings

Use the “New visual workflow finding” issue template. Start in `contribution-log/` or `failures/`. Promote a pattern into `prompt-library/` or `workflows/` after it succeeds in more than one relevant task.
