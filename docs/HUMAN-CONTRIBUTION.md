# What `maitranilim` contributed

The strongest contribution is a visual control system built through repeated supervision.

## Contribution pattern

| Human move | Why it matters | Reusable output |
|---|---|---|
| Defines the visual job and audience | Prevents style from replacing purpose | Brief schema |
| Separates invariants from desired changes | Makes reference editing testable | Preservation locks |
| Names composition precisely | Reduces camera and framing drift | Composition fragments |
| Demands believable texture and lighting | Counters over-processed AI aesthetics | Naturalism checks |
| Critiques the delta, not the entire image | Prevents unnecessary regeneration | Delta-edit loop |
| Converts failures into “do not change” rules | Makes the next attempt cheaper and safer | Negative constraints |
| Saves successful fragments by function | Reuses control without copying a full prompt | Prompt library |
| Selects and rejects outputs | Establishes a human acceptance layer | Quality rubric |
| Excludes personal and rights-sensitive sources | Makes public sharing safer | Privacy boundary |

## The teacher-student analogy

Across the recoverable history, `maitranilim` repeatedly taught the model what “same,” “natural,” “local,” and “believable” needed to mean in practice.

Examples include:

- keep the face, structure, skin tone, angle, and composition stable;
- preserve the original camera logic instead of inventing a new shot;
- make a requested text or object edit local;
- retain phone-camera realism rather than polished studio perfection;
- reduce highlight intensity, saturation, or synthetic smoothness when the image feels fake;
- protect the dominant subject and meaningful background anchors during cropping.

These are not claims of manual pixel work. They are evidence of **multimodal art direction**, **evaluation design**, and **reference-preserving workflow design**.

## Recruiter interpretation

For an AI art team, this shows an ability to convert taste into controllable language. For product marketing, it shows a repeatable briefing and review system. For design, it shows preservation, hierarchy, and failure-analysis discipline across different visual categories.
