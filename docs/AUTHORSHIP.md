# How I made this

The images here were generated or edited with AI image tools. I didn't draw or paint them by hand.

My part was everything around the pixels: the brief, the references, what had to stay untouched, the art direction, the critique, what got rejected, the prompt fragments I kept, and what ended up public. I also organized and wrote up the workflow.

## What I don't claim

- I didn't hand-illustrate any of these images.
- I didn't train or fine-tune a model. Repeated feedback made the results better within a session and grew my own library of instructions. That isn't the same as changing the model.

## What counts as my contribution

I treat a change as mine when it moves one of these:

- the problem or the audience
- what the reference is and what must be preserved
- composition or hierarchy
- style, palette, material, or finish
- how I judge whether it's done
- the smallest fix that repairs a failure
- a workflow I can reuse
- whether something is private or public
