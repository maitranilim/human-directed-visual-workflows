# Privacy and rights

## Public-release boundary

This repository excludes personal photographs. It also excludes an output that may preserve identity from a personal reference.

## Source-image checklist

Before publication:

- confirm who owns the source;
- confirm that depicted people consent to public use where required;
- remove location or device metadata when relevant;
- avoid exposing private surroundings or documents;
- check whether a reference is copyrighted, licensed, or only privately viewable;
- treat a transformed personal portrait as personal data, even if stylized.

## Template and reference preservation

The meme category is text-only because publishing a third-party template would create a rights question unrelated to the workflow. The method can be shared without redistributing the image.

## Brands and public causes

AI-generated posters may include names, marks, or implied affiliations. A concept image is not evidence of authorization or endorsement. Verify:

- trademark use;
- factual copy;
- organizational affiliation;
- cultural and religious details;
- accessibility and audience sensitivity.

## AI output rights

The legal status of AI-generated output varies. License grants in this repository cover only rights that `maitranilim` controls. Users remain responsible for their jurisdiction, source rights, publicity rights, and intended commercial use.
