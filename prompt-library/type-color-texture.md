# Type, color, and texture

## Typography fragments

Choose by function:

- condensed futuristic headline;
- wide brutalist display type;
- fragmented stencil lettering;
- modular type system;
- industrial warning-label type;
- micro-label UI text;
- vertical technical text blocks;
- digital terminal type;
- restrained glitch lettering.

Example:

```text
Use one oversized condensed headline, a small modular information grid,
and sparse technical micro-labels. Keep body copy conventionally readable.
```

Do not ask an image model to typeset final legal, factual, or product copy. Use the generated hierarchy as a concept, then recreate exact text in a deterministic design tool.

| Direction | Palette |
|---|---|
| Circuit editorial | Black, mint, cyan, violet, circuit green |
| Military editorial | Olive gray, burnt orange, dark leather black |
| Warning system | Black, white, warning red, steel gray |
| Neon noir | Magenta, deep blue shadow, hot pink rim, electric yellow accent |
| Natural phone image | Controlled highlights, believable white balance, restrained saturation |

Use color roles:

```text
Background: [color].
Primary subject: [color/material].
Functional accent: [color] used only for [meaning].
Temperature relationship: [cool background, warm subject].
```

## Naturalism controls

For believable edits:

- retain real skin or material texture;
- lower clipped highlights;
- keep saturation below “advertising gloss” unless required;
- match noise and sharpness to the source;
- preserve the direction and softness of light;
- avoid uniform smoothing;
- keep minor asymmetry and camera imperfection.

Example:

```text
Use mild dehaze and contrast, lower highlights, keep warm and cool regions
distinct, add only subtle sharpening and noise reduction, and finish with a
light vignette. Avoid fake saturation, plastic texture, and studio-perfect light.
```
