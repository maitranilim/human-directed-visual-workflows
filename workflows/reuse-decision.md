# Workflow: Reuse decision

## Reuse a fragment when

- it controls one clear visual function;
- the same failure can occur in another task;
- its wording is not tied to a private subject;
- it has succeeded in more than one relevant context;
- known conflicts are recorded.

## Reuse a whole prompt when

- the deliverable, audience, canvas, and content are genuinely similar;
- all preservation locks are revalidated;
- exact text and rights information are replaced;
- style is not being copied only because it once looked good.

## Do not reuse when

- the fragment embeds another person’s identity or private context;
- the output depends on a copyrighted reference that is not available for the new use;
- the prompt contains incompatible styles;
- a historical success cannot be distinguished from luck;
- the model or tool has changed enough to require a fresh test.

## Archive fields

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Fragment name | `local-text-only-edit` |
| Function | Preserve everything except one caption |
| Prevents | Whole-image drift |
| Works in | Posters, memes, UI mockups |
| Conflicts with | Full layout redesign |
| Evidence | Two accepted outputs or a logged comparison |
